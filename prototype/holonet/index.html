Holonet for ZenOS

A galaxy-far-away skin for the ZenOS App Launcher on jailbroken Kindles, drawn in the same black, gray and white language as Daily Games, so it stays crisp on e-ink.

  • Sabacc is built in: Corellian Spike rules against three table regulars, with spike dice and Sabacc Shifts.
  • Daily Ops is the Daily Games hub, restyled as a mission board.
  • The other tiles are mock launcher apps: library, files, ZenPM, Wi-Fi, clock and settings.
  • Tap the power button on the top edge, or the Hyperspace tile, for the sleep screen.
  • Ship Systems has a night shift (inverted e-ink) and the full-refresh flash toggle.

Unofficial fan prototype. Not affiliated with Lucasfilm or Disney.
